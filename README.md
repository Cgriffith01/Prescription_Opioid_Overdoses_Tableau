# Prescription Opioid Overdoses Are More Serious Than We Thought

A Tableau story built from CDC and Delaware overdose death data, walking through the scope of prescription opioid overdose deaths at both the state and national level.

## Files

- **Week_7_Tableau_Assignment_-_Christine_Trujillo.twbx** - the packaged Tableau workbook. Contains the full story, all dashboard sheets, and the underlying data extracts, opens directly in Tableau (Public or Desktop) with no separate data files needed.
- **Week_7_Tableau_Assignment_-_Christine_Trujillo.pdf** - a static export of the story, useful for a quick look or for submitting somewhere that can't render a live Tableau file.

## What it contains

The story is a four-part walkthrough:

1. **3 Counties in Delaware** - a map of New Castle, Kent, and Sussex counties, showing overdose deaths climbed 8.48% from the previous year, with New Castle County at roughly double the other two counties.
2. **Overdose deaths by type, 2014-2019** - a national trend comparing cocaine and prescription opioid overdose deaths over time, noting prescription opioid deaths had a slight decrease by 2019 but still totaled over 14,000.
3. **Rate of prescription overdose deaths by race, 2013-2019** - shows White and American Indian or Alaska Native populations sitting above the average death rate, which is over 4 per year across the period.
4. **Rate of prescription overdose deaths by sex, 2013-2019** - shows male overdose death rates running at roughly twice the rate of females, consistently across the years shown.

## Data sources

Packaged inside the .twbx file:

- `OD Rates Sex and Race.xlsx`
- `OverdoseDeaths_1999_2019.xlsx`
- `Week 3 Tableau CDC Data.csv`
- `Delaware_OverdoseDeaths_2019_Module4.xlsx`

These are CDC overdose death datasets plus a Delaware-specific county breakdown for 2019.

## Requirements

- Tableau Public or Tableau Desktop to open and interact with the .twbx file
- No additional data connections needed, everything is packaged into the workbook

## Usage

Open the .twbx file in Tableau to view the interactive story, click through the four story points at the top to move between the map, the trend chart, and the two demographic breakdowns. Use the PDF if you just need a static, shareable version of the same content.
